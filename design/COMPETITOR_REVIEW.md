# Competitor and reference review

Pages read on 12 October 2026. "Observed" means seen on the linked page that day; anything else is marked as an assumption. These products are references, not direct competitors: none of them routes a plain transfer into a third-party contract on Arc.

| Product | What it does well | What Doorstep can learn | What Doorstep should not copy | Opportunity for differentiation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Stripe Payment Links](https://stripe.com/payments/payment-links) | Observed: a five-word hero ("Create a link. Sell anywhere."), a demo showing the link in use, a three-step how-it-works, a live configurator that previews the result, tabbed code in seven languages. | Show the product working before explaining it; let visitors change inputs and see the output; keep code in tabs next to a short explanation. | Testimonials, analyst badges and coverage counts: Doorstep has none and must not imitate them. The chat-message demo composition. | The configurator idea, but the output is a real, recomputable address, not a preview URL. |
| [Coinbase Business / Commerce](https://www.coinbase.com/commerce) | Observed: a broad feature list with a "Trusted & secure" block and an FAQ. | Put safety in its own named section and answer plain questions in an FAQ. | Observed: unsourced headline statistics, and no visible checkout or status on the page. Doorstep should show real status and source every number. | Transparent, linkable evidence (contract, transactions) instead of claims. |
| [Request Finance](https://www.requestfinance.com/) | Observed: a problem section ("Systems that don't talk"), operations broken into named tasks, a dedicated safety section (SOC 2, insurance), a calculator, onboarding in three steps. | Name the problem in the customer's words before the solution; invoices described as operations (part paid, remaining). | Volume and customer counts, insurance and compliance badges: Doorstep has none. | A narrower, sharper promise: one integration problem solved completely. |
| [Arc](https://www.arc.io/) | Observed: developer resources (docs, explorer, faucet) one click away; technical labelling in the visual language. | Link the explorer for every claim; speak Arc's vocabulary (USDC gas, chain 5042). | Partner logo walls and institutional quotes. Arc's own visual identity. | Be the Arc-native answer to "how do I take payments from exchanges". |

## Conclusions used in the redesign

1. Show, then tell: the hero is an animated payment route with a refund branch, followed by an inspector that computes a real address in the browser.
2. Every number is either from the chain (live feed, explorer links) or from a test command in the README.
3. Safety and limitations get a full section, including "not audited".
4. Developer path within one scroll: code tabs and API reference link.
